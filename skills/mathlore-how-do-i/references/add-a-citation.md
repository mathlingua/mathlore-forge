# How Do I Add A Citation Or Source?

1. Search for an existing `[$resource]` definition by title, author, and key.
2. If found, cite it under `References:`. If absent, verify bibliographic details
   from authoritative information before adding a
   [`Resource:`](../../mathlingua-structural-language/references/resource.md).
3. Read [citations](../../mathlore-prose-content/references/citations.md) for PDF
   page offsets and placement.

```text
References:
. $example.book:page{42}
```

Keep the citation near the structured item or prose it supports.

## Do not

- Do not invent missing bibliographic metadata.
- Do not create a duplicate resource key.
- Do not cite a page without checking the source's numbering and `offset:`.
- Do not add a citation that does not support the claim.
