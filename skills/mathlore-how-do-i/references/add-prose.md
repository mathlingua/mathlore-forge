# How Do I Add Explanatory Prose?

1. Read the entire page and neighboring pages for voice, vocabulary, and
   notation.
2. Read [narrative prose](../../mathlore-prose-content/references/narrative-prose.md)
   and [outline groups](../../mathlingua-structural-language/references/outline-and-prose-groups.md).
3. Add a `Text:` block that supplies motivation, intuition, transition, or
   interpretation not already obvious from the structured item.

```text
Text: "The identity element is the unique element that leaves every group element unchanged under multiplication."
```

Use a section title only when the page's conceptual structure needs a visible
subdivision. Run the checker to review reflow and IDs.

## Do not

- Do not restate the next card word for word.
- Do not introduce terminology after using it.
- Do not hide reusable formal content in prose.
- Do not add unsupported historical or mathematical claims.
