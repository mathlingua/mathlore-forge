# How Do I Add Or Change Notation?

Choose the mechanism by scope and meaning:

- Command notation: `Documented: written:` with
  [rendering templates](../../mathlore-prose-content/references/rendering-templates.md).
- Mapping invocation/form notation: `Documented: writing:`/`as:`.
- Plain-name rendering: top-level or local
  [`Writing:`](../../mathlingua-structural-language/references/writing.md).
- Semantic shorthand/reduction: [`Aliases:` and capability arrows](../../mathlingua-formulation-language/references/aliases-and-capabilities.md).

Example command notation:

```text
Documented:
. called: "function from $A?$ to $B?$"
. written: "A? \\rightarrow B?"
```

Search for existing notation and inspect the rendered cards after checking.

## Do not

- Do not use rendering aliases to establish semantics.
- Do not add a global alias for a local need.
- Do not reference placeholders absent from the heading.
- Do not infer source command syntax from the notation users see.
