# Narrative Prose

Use `Text:` for exposition that motivates, interprets, or connects structured
mathematics.

```text
Text: "A group packages a set with an associative operation, an identity, and inverses."
```

Read the whole page first. Match its terminology, notation, capitalization,
voice, and pacing. Prefer concise mathematical sentences. Introduce terminology
before relying on it, and use inline `$LaTeX$` where it improves readability.
Alternate prose and structured cards in the style established by the chapter.

When mathematical content is uncertain, verify it from an authoritative source
or state the uncertainty. `mlg check` verifies structure and tracked facts; it
does not prove statements.

## Anti-patterns

- Do not merely paraphrase the formula immediately below.
- Do not bury a reusable definition or theorem entirely in prose.
- Do not invent historical claims or citations.
- Do not claim a clean check proves mathematical truth.
